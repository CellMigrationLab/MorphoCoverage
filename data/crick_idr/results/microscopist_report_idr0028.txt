What this dataset cannot tell me
  - Every question was assessed, each on the columns and under the assumptions stated with it and in the notes below.
  - Every answer holds for the stated features, tolerances and definition of new.
  - Replays, draws and hold-outs describe these samples; they are not independent experiments. Statements across samples count samples or repeats.
  - A field count that preserved this acquisition is not a protocol until it holds on samples it was not chosen on.
  - Technical drift between repeats, batches or plates is not assessed: groups are analysed apart, and a shift shared by all fields of a group is not new within it.
