  ➖ 1. Can the features see the phenotype? — not assessed; needs a positive control: a condition
     known to change the phenotype
  ➖ 10. Groups of tracks? — no group: no candidate group passed the check on these features
  ❌ Data integrity: the checks in the overview name 12 fields in 6 findings (fields that hold
     another field's observations, or their own twice), and every answer counts them as independent
     fields.
  Assumptions: Every answer holds for the stated features, tolerances and definition of new.
    Replays, draws and hold-outs describe these samples; they are not independent experiments.
    Statements across samples count samples or repeats. A field count that preserved this
    acquisition is not a protocol until it holds on samples it was not chosen on. No representation
    control: a 'nothing new' answer says nothing about phenotypes these features and this definition
    of new cannot see. Technical drift between repeats, batches or plates is not assessed: groups
    are analysed apart, and a shift shared by all fields of a group is not new within it. Each
    'enough' is per technical repeat; the condition mean also varies between technical repeats,
    which more fields of one technical repeat cannot reduce (question 3; optimise, question 7: more
    fields or more technical repeats?). The Foundation, the stopping rules and the inspection's
    influence and neighbour reasons read the readout (Mean Speed) only; the other declared features
    (Median Speed, Speed Standard Deviation, Mean Speed Rolling, Median Speed Rolling, Max Speed
    Rolling and 7 more) enter novelty, the unusual tracks and the feature ranking, so a heavy tail
    or a between-well spread in them is not judged by any stopping rule. No stage coordinates:
    neighbouring fields were not compared, and spatial redundancy and dispersed orders were not
    assessed. No acquisition order: the recorded order was not replayed, and the random orders treat
    the fields as exchangeable, so a drift or a raster along the acquisition is not seen.

Settings: 'Did I image enough?' replays each rule in 5 random orders (seeded from random_state); random_state 0.
