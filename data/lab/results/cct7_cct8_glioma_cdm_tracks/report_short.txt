What this dataset cannot tell me

  ❌ Data integrity: the checks in the overview name 12 fields in 6 findings (fields that hold
     another field's observations, or their own twice), and every answer counts them as independent
     fields.
  ⚠️ No representation control: a 'nothing new' answer says nothing about phenotypes these features
