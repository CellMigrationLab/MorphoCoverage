Before the analysis
  1. What should the analysis answer: compare the population averages of the conditions, compare
     their phenotypic fingerprints, or check a rare population? Suggested: averages.
     averages: the readout's mean per condition across biological repeats (the default);
     fingerprint: how several features vary together, the covariance fingerprint, declared as
     fingerprint=(OutcomeCriterion(...), ...); rare: whether a rare population was sampled enough,
     declared as rare=RareTarget(...).
  2. Which feature is the readout you report? Options: Area, Perim., Major, Minor, Circ., Feret,
     MinFeret, AR and 2 more.
     Every answer is about the readout; the other features describe the phenotype.
  Then: setup = scan.setup(readout=..., goal=...) and report(scan.objects, scan.fovs, setup).
