    approximate: the leave-one-field-out outcomes share their reference fields. The novelty bounds
    treat fields as independent, representative draws: neighbouring fields acquired in a raster over
    patchy states make them too low. Every novelty bound holds at a number of fields fixed in
    advance, not at the first field where it passes: stopping there is premature more often than 5%

  Assumptions: The paired t-test on the repeat means treats each biological repeat as one replicate:
    it keeps false positives at 5%, but with 3 biological repeats only large, consistent effects
    reach q ≤ 0.05. q: Benjamini-Hochberg within each sub-experiment; 'significant across repeats'
    uses the normalised q. The false discovery rate is controlled within each family, not across the
    report. Reagents of one target that go the same way in every repeat speak against an off-target
    effect of one of them; this is described, not tested, since they share the control. The
    cell-level test treats the cells as independent, so it asks whether these wells differ, not
    whether the effect repeats. Normalised: each value divided by its own repeat's control mean.
    Balanced: the median d over 100 draws with as many cells from each repeat, its p from labels
    shuffled within each repeat. Within a repeat the conditions are described by their effect sizes;
    they are tested across repeats (the first table). The test and the effect sizes compare means: a
    difference confined to a subpopulation of cells or to the shape of the distribution is not
    tested (see the unusual cells). Repeats needed: for a paired t-test at p ≤ 0.05/m, m the
    conditions of the sub-experiment (what Benjamini-Hochberg asks at most), on the values that test
    compares: each repeat's change (its mean over its control's, a fold change; the difference of
    the means for a feature with a value that is not positive; the table gives their geometric mean,
    as the verdict does), its spread between the repeats and its sampling error within one. All
    projected from the repeats here, so the plan is a guide.
