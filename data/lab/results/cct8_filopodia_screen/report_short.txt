  Assumptions: Across biological repeats, the paired t-test on the repeat means (as in SuperPlots)
    treats each biological repeat as one replicate: it keeps false positives at 5%, but with 3

    described, not tested, since they share the control. The cell-level test treats the cells as

    'without an effect' column says how often it would be positive with no effect at the variation
    between repeats seen here. Normalised: each value divided by the mean of its own repeat's
    control. Balanced: the median over 100 draws with the same number of cells from each repeat,
